## Method

- Sun altitude from the Krios ephemeris for the rotating oblate Earth; sunrise/sunset when the geometric altitude of the Sun's centre crosses -0.833 deg (34' refraction + 16' semi-diameter); local solar transit by a parabola through the altitude maximum.
- Residual = observed - predicted; combined uncertainty = sqrt(observer^2 + model^2), where the model term (default 90 s) reflects horizon refraction and terrain.

## Assumptions and limits

- Flat, unobstructed horizon at sea level; standard atmosphere. Hills or buildings shift sunrise/sunset by minutes.
- Times in UTC (add the zone offset to local clock readings); longitudes east positive.
- Noon altitude is the maximum of the geometric altitude; apparent altitude adds <= 0.02 deg of refraction at high Sun.
